How these are drawn

Every model is procedural, built in kosmo-models.js — the same renderer and shader toolkit that draws the Kosmopedia nebulae. The cytoplasm is the nebula's layered fbm cloud; the membrane is a fresnel shell.

Sizes are typical ranges, not measurements of these drawings, and the cells are not to scale with each other (a virus is a hundred times smaller than an egg).

Credits

Reference links go to Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0), by its contributors; the drawings are changed, generated illustrations. Every cell is labelled with the article its figures come from.

Cells are the unit of life, and they are not one thing: a bacterium has no nucleus, a plant cell builds a wall and a vacuole, a neuron is a metre of wiring, an egg is a single cell you can see. Open any entry to take the membrane off and look inside.
