What the records can count about the people a tradition recognised — and what they cannot

Controls

The registers, the censuses and the survey are published counts. The branching rate is arithmetic on a register. The reach per teacher is an assumption, and only the last two charts move when you drag it.

Recognition, not realisation. Nothing on this page counts enlightened people, because nothing can. What can be counted is recognition: the names a tradition wrote into its own transmission list, the people living inside a monastery when a census was taken, and the adults who told a survey they practised. Those are three different instruments measuring three different things. The page keeps them apart, computes what follows from them, and marks the step where the evidence stops: no survey has a denominator for persistent nondual awareness.

Enlightened fallout

Define fallout as the people a recognised teacher actually trained. It is computable from the register's growth and one assumption — how many students one teacher takes in a life. The first chart is that product over the Chan register's 472 years. The second is the only place the page can put a number on nondual awareness today, and it is a band, not a point.
