Controls

Every line here is a seven-day mean of daily transit calls reported by the IMF's PortWatch programme. Capacity is the carrying capacity of the ships that passed, not the tonnage of cargo they carried.

Why this page exists. Every other page in this lab moves in years or decades. A strait closes in an afternoon. Food and fuel stop moving weeks before they stop being produced, and the 28 passages below are where that happens: a handful of gaps between seas that carry most of the world's manufactured goods and a large share of its energy. This page is here to keep the slow models honest about how fast the fast layer moves.
