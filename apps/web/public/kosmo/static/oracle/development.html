Six maps of how minds and societies grow up. One of them has an instrument

Controls

Everything on this page is either measured or cited, and the two are never mixed in one chart. The measured charts come from a Seshat release fetched at build time. The asserted ones — every colour on the ladder, every altitude — are drawn as dashes, because there is no instrument behind them.

The problem with developmental models is that they are all partly true. Spiral Dynamics, integral theory, Loevinger's ego stages, Kohlberg's moral stages, Kegan's orders and the Lectical scale all describe something real — that meaning-making gets more complex in an order that cannot easily be reversed. But only three of the six have an instrument, only two have published reliability on a sample anyone has described, and none of them has a population estimate. This page separates the ladder from the measurement, and puts the measurable history of complexity next to it.
