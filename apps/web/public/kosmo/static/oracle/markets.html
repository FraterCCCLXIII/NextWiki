Credit, rates and the price of expectations — the channel that turns a bad quarter into a contraction

Controls

Monthly means of daily and weekly series from the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis. Spreads are Moody's seasoned corporate yields less the 10-year Treasury; nothing here is interpolated across a gap.

Why this page exists. The Dark Factory model has no debt, no interest rate and no credit spread in it anywhere — an audit of the code found the words do not occur. That is a real absence, because a falling index is not how a market shock reaches the economy. It reaches it through margin calls, covenant breaches, collateral calls and a contraction in lending, which cuts spending, which cuts earnings, which widens spreads again. That loop is the amplifier in every modern equity crash, and it is what this page measures.
