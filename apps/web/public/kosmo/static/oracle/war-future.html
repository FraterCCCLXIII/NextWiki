War, to 10,000

The only page here that forecasts war. It forecasts a distribution, and the distribution is decided by one exponent

Levers

Every control here is labelled by where its number comes from. The onset rate and the tail exponent are measured and published; the deterrence, escalation and settlement numbers are judgment, because no published effect size exists for any of them. Moving a judgment lever moves the future, which is the point of a scenario.

What this page does, and what it refuses to do. It does not forecast when a war will happen. It cannot: no one has ever predicted one at a horizon longer than a few months, and the scored record of geopolitical forecasting gets to chance within about five years. What it does instead is take the one part of war that has been measured over two centuries — how often a war starts, and how big it is once it starts — and runs it forward, so the question becomes what would have to be true for the next eight thousand years to be peaceful. That question has an answer.
