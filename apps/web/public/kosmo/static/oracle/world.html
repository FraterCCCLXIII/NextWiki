Controls

The year control moves one frame of measured data, not an interpolation between frames: before 1950 the panel is decadal because the underlying reconstruction is. Population sizes the bubbles and weights every average here.

Why this page exists. "World GDP per capita is $17,000" and "world life expectancy is 74" are both true and both describe nobody. The same planet holds countries at $700 a head and at $120,000, with life expectancies of 52 and 85. The models on the other pages run on the average, and the average is exactly the thing that hides who gets displaced, who pays for the transfers, and which countries have the fiscal room to absorb a shock that arrives in five years rather than fifty.
