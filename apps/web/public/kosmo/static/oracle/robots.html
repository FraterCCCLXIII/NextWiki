Levers

The stock to 2025 is measured (IFR World Robotics). The price curve is anchored to published unit prices. Everything after 2025 is arithmetic on those anchors plus the levers above — and the ownership lever has no measured global series at all, which is why it is the one worth moving.

One number on this page is measured and one is a choice. How many robots exist, and what one costs, are published figures. Who owns them is not: there is no global register of robot ownership, so the split is a lever. That matters because the model says the ownership split dominates every physical parameter — cheaper robots change the size of the pie, ownership changes who eats.
