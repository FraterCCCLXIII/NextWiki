Capacity

State capacity, fiscal room and legitimacy — the variable that decides whether a shock becomes a collapse

Controls

World Bank Worldwide Governance Indicators, plus tax, debt and income from the World Development Indicators. Perception-based estimates in normal units — comparable across countries in a year, only loosely across years.

Why this page exists. Every forecast in this lab ends with the same unstated assumption: that somebody capable is in charge. Two countries can take an identical shock — a war, a pandemic, an AI-driven collapse in white-collar work — and one absorbs it while the other comes apart. The difference is not in any model here. It is whether the state can tax, staff, procure, count its own population and be obeyed. That is what these six perception indices measure, and it is the largest hole the audit found.
