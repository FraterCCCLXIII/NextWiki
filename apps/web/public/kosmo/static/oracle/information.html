What the world reads, what it stopped reading, and how much of the reading is now about the machine

Controls

GDELT measures the share of every monitored news article that matches a phrase, so it is a share and not a count. Wikimedia's figures are human views only, bots excluded. Neither source is a sample.

Why this page exists. The Dark Factory model turns on expectations, and expectations are made of what people read. The audit of that model found no term for attention, trust or narrative anywhere in it — belief enters as a single smoothing constant. That is the largest missing amplifier after credit, because a job loss is a fact and a wave of job losses is a story, and the story is what moves the spending that the model is trying to predict. This page measures the story: how much of the world's news is about the machine, how much of the world's reading follows it, how free that reading is, and how much of it anybody believes.
