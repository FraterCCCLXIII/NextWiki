WHAT “OPTIMAL” MEANS HERE

Prioritize populated destinations, use direct links when possible, and add off-axis routing for conjunction resilience. Extra relays do not shorten the light-speed distance. An actual optimum needs a traffic matrix, antenna sizes, transmitter power, target data rates, reliability and cost constraints.

Use X/Ka-band high-gain links for the backbone and disruption-tolerant store-and-forward routing. Inner-system relays can use solar power; distant hubs need a power architecture suited to weak sunlight.

MODEL LIMITS

Circular coplanar orbits with illustrative initial phases, not live ephemerides. Positions and route delays change with the time slider. Local occultations, scheduling, processing delay, antenna pointing, link capacity and relay failures are excluded. “Entire solar system” here means gateways out to 50 AU, not continuous access at every point or the distant Oort cloud.
