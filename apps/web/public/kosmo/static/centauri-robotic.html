Centauri Fleet: v5, the ship that flies itself

The crewed classes rebuilt around an AI swarm and a robot workforce. The ship pilots itself; a small human cadre sleeps in cyclic torpor and is woken by tiered triggers; an observation deck lets the awake watch telepresence into the robots and scrub through everything the swarm recorded. Every number below is measured from the placed parts in parts/robotic.js; the argument is in CENTAURI-ROBOTIC.md.
—

1 · Before and after

The three crewed classes in every version, measured by the same solver. The v2 and v3 columns show the ships as pinned and with the consumables and life support their own crews would need under the laws v5 uses, because a ship that forgot to eat is not a fair baseline.

2 · The trade study

Six routes scored one to five against a weighted rubric. The uncrewed ship scores highest and the study says so: the recommendation is the best route that meets the requirement the crewed classes exist for, that adults arrive alive and able to act; the one that beats it is built as the hedge.

3 · The robots

The inventory for the selected ship, every row a placed part with a flown analogue. Counts come from the hull: crawlers per metre of rail, inspectors per square metre of hull, humanoids per module and per ten sleepers.
