## Explanation

Let a ball go. It falls. Something pulls it down. That pull is called gravity.

Gravity pulls on everything. It pulls on you, on your shoes, and on this book. Earth is very big, so its pull is strong. That is why we stay on the ground and do not float away.

Weight is the pull of gravity on an object. When you hold a heavy bag, you feel the pull. The bag presses down on your hand. A heavier bag is pulled harder, so it presses harder.

We can see weight with a spring scale. A spring scale has a spring inside. When you hang something on it, gravity pulls the object down. The spring stretches. The more the spring stretches, the bigger the pull.

A rubber band works in a similar way. Hang a small bag on it and it stretches a little. Hang a heavier bag and it stretches more.

Other worlds pull differently. The Moon is smaller than Earth. Its gravity is weaker. On the Moon, the same rock is pulled down less. It weighs less. The rock is still the same rock. It has the same amount of stuff in it. Only the pull is different.

An astronaut on the Moon can jump high. The pull is weak, so it is easy to lift up. On a bigger world, with stronger gravity, jumping would be hard.

## Worked example

Think about a bag of apples. You hang it from a rubber band on Earth. The band stretches a lot.

Now imagine the same bag on the Moon. The Moon pulls less. The band would stretch less. The apples did not change. There are the same apples in the bag. But the pull on them is smaller, so the weight is smaller.

On Earth: strong pull, long stretch. On the Moon: weaker pull, short stretch.

## Activity

You need: a rubber band, a paper clip, a small paper bag or cup with a handle, a ruler, and some small things to put inside, such as coins, erasers or marbles. Use a grown-up helper.

1. Ask a helper to hold the rubber band by one end so it hangs down. Keep it low, near a table.
2. Hook the bag onto the other end with the paper clip. Keep your feet clear of the floor under it.
3. Measure how long the band is with the bag empty. Write it down.
4. Add one eraser. Measure again.
5. Keep adding things, one at a time. Measure each time.
6. Look at your list of numbers. What happens as the bag gets heavier?

Talk about it: What is pulling the bag down? What would happen to the stretch on the Moon?

## Summary

- Gravity is a pull that draws things toward each other.
- Weight is the pull of gravity on an object.
- We feel weight as pressing down. A spring scale or rubber band shows it by stretching.
- The Moon pulls less than Earth, so things weigh less there. They still have the same amount of stuff.
